Privacy Policy — Farkle

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version

Farkle is a dice game. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your games, stats and settings stay on your phone, and the app does not send anything anywhere.

Online play is coming in a later update. When it arrives, an online game will be the only thing that leaves the phone, and only if you choose to play one. The "Online games" section below says what it will send, and this policy will be updated with a new date before that happens.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your device so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the game in progress, for pass-and-play, against the computer and in the scorekeeper
  • the players and rules you last used, and any rule sets you saved
  • your stats: the results of finished games
  • your settings, such as theme, vibration and keeping the screen on
  • a random ID for this install (16 letters and numbers, made on the phone, not linked to you or your device), kept for online play

None of this is sent anywhere. Settings has buttons to clear your stats and delete saved games, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Online games

Online play is not available yet; in the app it shows as "Coming soon", and right now nothing is sent. When it arrives it will be optional. When you create or join a room, the app will send the following to Google Firebase Realtime Database, the server that keeps the room in sync between phones:

  • the 4-letter room code
  • the name you type for yourself in that room (it can be anything; it does not have to be your real name)
  • the random install ID described above, so the room knows which seat is yours
  • the game settings, every move and score, and when they happened

Anyone who has the room code can see the room: the names, the seats and the game. Do not put anything private in your name.

Like any internet service, Firebase receives your phone's IP address when the app connects. Google handles that under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Rooms are kept on the server after a game ends and are not linked to who you are. You can have them deleted: see "Deleting your data" below.

Deleting your data

Farkle has no account, so there is nothing to sign in to or close. This is how to delete each kind of data.

On your phone (stats, saved games, settings, the install ID and the last room you joined):

  • In Farkle, open Settings, then Your data, and tap Clear stats or Delete saved games.
  • Or uninstall Farkle. That deletes everything the app saved on the phone.

Online games, once online play is available (the rooms on the server: room code, the names, install IDs, moves and scores):

  • Email bragi.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Farkle data".
  • Include the code of each room you want deleted. The code of your last room is shown in Farkle under Play online, as "Your game" followed by the code. If you don't have the code, send the name you used and roughly when you played.
  • The rooms are deleted completely within 30 days and you get a reply when it's done. No copy is kept.

What the app does not do

  • no sign-in and no account
  • no ads and no ad IDs
  • no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind
  • no purchases
  • no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • no selling or sharing of data with anyone

Children

Farkle is a game for everyone. It does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only text a player types is a name for a game, which stays on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: bragi.support@gmail.com
